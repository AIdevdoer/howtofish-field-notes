The How to Fish companion has two checklists. One tracks story stages. The other tracks achievements. Both save the boxes you tick in the browser you are using.

These notes document that behavior. The companion is an independent player tool for Dazed Games' Steam game How to Fish. It is not affiliated with Dazed Games or Valve.

## A tick is a note you made

Open the [How to Fish achievement tracker](https://howtofish.ing/achievements/), search an achievement name or choose a category, then tick the achievements already unlocked in your game. The checklist displays your marked total.

Ticking a box does not unlock a Steam achievement. Leaving it empty does not change an achievement you already earned. Compare the checklist with Steam before treating an empty box as unfinished game content.

The story checklist needs a slightly different habit. Mark a stage after the relevant boss drop has been handed back to the NPC. A defeated boss and a finished hand-in are different points in that route. Checking the stage too soon can hide the errand that opens the next destination.

## Same browser, same notes

The checklists use browser-local storage. They are separate lists, so marking a story stage does not automatically mark a Steam achievement in the companion.

A second browser or device can show an empty list even when the first one has checked boxes. Clearing this site's browser data can also remove those notes. If you use a private window, do not rely on its checklist surviving after that session closes.

Before switching devices or clearing site data, write down the checked stages and achievement names, or keep a screenshot. There is no account sync described by these checklists, and they are not a backup of your game save.

## Reset with a reason

Each checklist has its own Reset control and asks for confirmation. Use it when you want to clear that list's marked entries, such as when tracking another run. The reset belongs to the companion checklist. It does not reset Steam achievements or the game's save file.

If progress appears to disappear, first check whether you changed browsers, devices, profiles, or private sessions. That is a storage question before it is a game-progression question.
